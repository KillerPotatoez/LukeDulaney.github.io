Save or share

Your results live only in this browser. Save a copy of the file if you clear your browser, change phones, or want to send it to another parent or carer.

This page does not give medical advice. It only does the arithmetic on numbers you enter and shows them on a graph. Bone age results must be interpreted by your child's doctor, who will weigh them alongside growth, puberty stage and your child's diagnosis. What counts as expected varies a great deal between conditions.

Your privacy. Everything you type stays in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent to a server, there is no account, and no one else can see it — including whoever made this page. Clearing your browser data will erase it, so keep a backup file.
